+385% Post-2012 False Surge

Because VIIRS 375m has a 9× smaller pixel area and detects fires down to 0.5 MW, raw hotspot counts jump dramatically in 2012. Fire managers misinterpret this as a catastrophic climate surge rather than a sensor change.
