📅 1. Multi-Decadal Burning Activity Calendar

Cheng 2020 & Paramanik 2019
Innovation: Transforms over 23 years (2002–2024) of active satellite fire observations into an interactive Day-of-Year (DOY 1–365) heatmap matrix with continuous fire radiative energy ramps. Includes a 20-year climatological baseline envelope (10th, Median 50th, 90th, 95th Extreme percentiles) that identifies seasonal onset, peak burning days, and cessation dates for any area of interest.
Backend Data Used: Historical MODIS C6.1 (`MCD14DL`) and VIIRS C2 (`VNP14IMGTDL` / `VJ114IMGTDL`) active fire records. Ingests latitude/longitude, acquisition timestamps, Day/Night flags, raw Fire Radiative Power (MW), and 20-year daily percentiles calculated via vectorized sliding-window estimators.

⚖️ 2. The "Sensor Transition Illusion" Diagnostic

Weng 2017 & Gao 2006
Innovation: Directly resolves the fundamental challenge problem: unharmonized raw records display an artificial +385% to +436% surge after 2012 caused purely by the deployment of VIIRS 375m detectors (which capture sub-pixel fires down to 0.5 MW). Pyro-Harmony normalizes this discrepancy using physics-based footprint scaling and ML cross-calibration to preserve true climate trends.
Backend Data Used: View Zenith Angle (VZA) scan and track parameters, satellite orbital altitudes (705 km Terra/Aqua vs 829 km S-NPP), and collocated Aqua-MODIS & S-NPP VIIRS matchups. Calculates pixel expansion ratios ($A(\theta)$ up to 10× in MODIS vs 2.5× in VIIRS) and Equivalent Standard Fire Pixels (ESFP).

🗺️ 3. Geospatial Hotspot Clustering & Live Streaming

STARFM (Gao 2006) DBSCAN
Innovation: Integrates an interactive dark operational GIS map displaying multi-sensor detections (cyan for MODIS 1km, fire-red for VIIRS 375m) and clustered physical fire event centroids (amber). Connects directly to live NASA FIRMS feeds for real-time 24-hour global hotspot ingestion and instantaneous on-the-fly harmonization.
Backend Data Used: Live NASA FIRMS open NRT CSV endpoints (`MODIS_C6_1_Global_24h.csv`, `SUOMI_VIIRS_C2_Global_24h.csv`, `J1_VIIRS_C2_Global_24h.csv`). Spatio-temporal DBSCAN features include projected coordinates ($x, y$ in km) and decimal acquisition hours scaled by 1.5 km spatial / 45-minute temporal tolerances.

📑 4. Incident Commander Wildfire Briefing

Zhang 2020 Biome Stratification
Innovation: Automated intelligence generator producing tactical wildfire briefings for emergency operations centers, land managers, and incident commanders. Flags critical burning periods (consecutive days with $Z \ge 2.0\sigma$), evaluates threat levels, and generates actionable recommendations for early aerial retardant staging and agricultural burn bans.
Backend Data Used: Fuel biome stratification (Forest, Agricultural Crop Residue, Savanna, Mediterranean Shrubland) following Zhang et al. (2020), 20-year climatological mean ($\mu$) and standard deviation ($\sigma$) per Day of Year, and consecutive anomaly streak tracking. Exportable to Markdown and clipboard.
